## 1. One-liner

A web app — a small SUITE of three modules sharing one attendee profile — for the **Germany Career
Summit 2026**. It gives each attendee an instant **5-dimension Germany Readiness Score** (Reality
Check), a **CV & LinkedIn gap diagnosis**, and an **AI-generated week-by-week 90-Day Roadmap**, then
enriches the lead and delivers results to WhatsApp. It powers the event's existing agenda and feeds the
backend coaching offer ("accountability sprint").

## 4. The three modules (one product, one shared profile)

1. **Reality Check** → the **5-dimension Germany Readiness Score**. Result page is structured as his
   worksheet components: **Profile Assessment, Skills Evaluation, Gap Analysis, Germany Readiness
   Score.** Pentagon radar. (= morning block + room aggregate.)
2. **CV & LinkedIn Lab** → paste/upload résumé + LinkedIn + target JD → **ATS match score, keyword
   gaps, weak-bullet flags**. DIAGNOSIS ONLY; humans do the fix. (= live CV transformation block.)
3. **90-Day Roadmap** → AI directional, **week-by-week** plan from the person's readiness profile +
   CV gaps, mirroring his physical "90-Day Visual Roadmap." (= 1:30 block + 3:00 Personal Action Plan.)

All three write to ONE Submission profile so they compound. This is also the **"Guided Mode" /
accountability-sprint** foundation (post-summit weekly nudges + re-scoring over time) — architect for
it, don't build it yet.

## 5. THE 5 DIMENSIONS (= the 5 workbook sections; matches the public "5-dimension" branding)

1. **Profile** — positioning, German CV, recruiter 10-sec read, measurable results
2. **Strategy** — targeted system, Mittelstand list, direct outreach, network, Xing
3. **German & Skills** — language level, speaking practice, provable technical skills
4. **Mindset** — response to rejection/silence, agency vs. blame, comparison
5. **90-Day Plan** — written weekly plan vs. hope; recent concrete action

The "5 Questions That Change Everything" capstone is reflective and **NOT scored** (it stays on paper).
Quiz questions are FRESH proxies — they must NOT copy the workbook's actual prompts, because attendees
answer the real workbook live at the event.

## 6. The 5 archetypes (by weakest dimension) — grounded in B2's own ad/page language

| Weakest dim | Archetype | False belief (the enemy) | Reframe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Profile | The Invisible Profile | "My experience speaks for itself." | German employers hire the problem you solve, visible in 10 seconds. |
| Strategy | The Spray-and-Pray Applier | "More applications = more chances." | A targeted system beats volume; blind applications go into a black hole. |
| German & Skills | The Cert Waiter | "I need B1/B2 before I start." | Speaking + provable skills open doors; waiting burns hiring cycles. |
| Mindset | The Stalled Believer | "Maybe Germany isn't for someone like me." | Results come from adjusting the approach — not luck or talent. |
| 90-Day Plan | The Planless Hoper | "I'll figure it out as I go / I just need more info." | A written weekly plan is the gap between another lost year and an offer. |

## 8. NON-NEGOTIABLE design principles

1. **AI surfaces & accelerates; the human understands & decides.** Modules diagnose (score, gaps, ATS
   read) and draft *directional* plans. NEVER output a finished résumé/cover letter or imply the coach
   is unnecessary. The live page itself says it's NOT for people who "want someone to do the work for
   you" — the tool must honour that.
2. **Additive to the paper kit, not a replacement.** The paper Germany Readiness Worksheet + 90-Day
   Visual Roadmap stay (honest, private, take-home keepsakes). The app earns its place ONLY by doing
   what paper can't: **COMPUTE** (real score), **AGGREGATE** (room view), **PERSONALIZE at scale**,
   **FOLLOW HOME** (WhatsApp). Do not digitize the deep reflective workbook — only the scoreable pulse.
3. **Never feel like "just another tool/vendor."** B2 positions against consultants who take money and
   change nothing. The app must visibly serve Ameen's inside-Germany method.
4. **Honest "mirror," never cruel.** Direct about gaps; always points to the fix (summit / sprint).
5. **Restrained, purposeful motion.** One orchestrated reveal (the radar drawing itself) over scattered
   effects. No gimmicky 3D that undercuts a "mirror."
6. **Match Ameen's exact public terms:** "Reality Check," "Germany Readiness Score" (5-dimension),
   "Germany Readiness Worksheet," "Live CV Transformation," "90-Day (Visual) Roadmap," "Personal Action
   Plan," "accountability sprint." (An earlier kit asset said "Market Readiness Score" — the live page
   says "Germany Readiness Score"; use that, confirm with Ameen.)

## 9. Tech stack & integrations (match the operator's existing stack)

- Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind. Docker + PM2 + Traefik on Hostinger VPS, Cloudflare DNS.
- Postgres + Prisma (SQLite local). The app needs its own store for the live aggregate.
- Anthropic API (default) via a server route behind an env-configurable provider adapter; GPT-4.1-mini
  is an acceptable alternative the operator already uses.
- Self-hosted **n8n** + **Evolution API** (WhatsApp) + **Google Sheets** (lightweight CRM). App POSTs
  results to an n8n webhook; n8n appends to Sheets (tagged by archetype/tier for placeability) and sends
  the WhatsApp takeaway via Evolution API. (n8n flow built separately by the operator.)
- **FlexiFunnels pass-through:** read `ff_name`, `ff_email`, `ff_phone` from URL params; skip capture,
  enrich the existing lead.
