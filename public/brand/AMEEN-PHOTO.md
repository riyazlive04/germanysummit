# Ameen hero portrait

The home hero has a portrait slot on the right (desktop). Drop a clean,
**background-removed** cutout of Ameen here:

- `ameen.png` — transparent background, head-to-torso, facing camera.

Best source: the front-facing "grey suit" photo, with the book and all marketing
text/graphics removed. A free 30-second route: upload the chosen photo to
remove.bg, download the transparent PNG, save it as `public/brand/ameen.png`.

Until the file exists, the hero shows a tasteful green panel with his name — so
nothing looks broken. Once `ameen.png` is present it fills the panel
automatically (no code change needed).
