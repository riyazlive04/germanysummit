# Your Germany Readiness Check
### For summit attendees

**In one line**
- Find out how ready you really are for a job in Germany, and what to fix first.

**Why bother (under 10 minutes)**
- You get a clear number, not a guess.
- You see the exact gap holding you back.
- You walk out with a 90-day plan, not just notes.

**What you will get**
- Your Germany Readiness Score, 0 to 100.
- A 5-point picture of where you are strong and where you are weak.
- Your archetype: the kind of candidate you are right now.
- An honest read of your CV and LinkedIn against what German recruiters actually look for.
- A week-by-week 90-day plan you can download.

**How to do it (3 steps)**
- Scan the QR, or open **germanysummit.sirahagents.com**
- **Step 1, Reality Check:** answer 10 honest questions, get your score.
- **Step 2, CV and LinkedIn Lab:** upload your CV, paste a German job you want, see what is missing.
- **Step 3, 90-Day Roadmap:** get your plan, download it, start week 1.

**See your results anytime**
- Use the same email every time so your results stay linked.
- Come back later at `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/my-results` to see everything in one place.

**Be honest with the questions**
- The score is only useful if your answers are. No one is grading you. The point is the truth.

**One honest note**
- This is a mirror and a map. It tells you where you stand and shows you the path.
- Walking it is on you. The accountability sprint with the coach is how most people actually finish.
