01

What it is, and why it exists

  • One link, three tools, one score. Every attendee gets a personal readiness profile keyed to their email.
  • The objective: move each person from inspiration to action while they are still in the room, in under 10 minutes.
  • It does not ask for stage time. It powers the blocks already on the agenda.
  • It feeds the back end: the app exposes the gap, the accountability sprint closes it.
The golden rule

The app diagnoses. The coach and the sprint deliver. It never writes a CV or hands over the finished answer. It shows the gap, then points to you. This is by design, and it protects the offer.

03

The three tools

Module 01 / Reality Check

A number they cannot argue with

  • 10 honest questions across 5 dimensions: Profile, Strategy, German & Skills, Mindset, 90-Day Plan.
  • Gives a 0 to 100 Germany Readiness Score, a pentagon radar, their archetype, and the one gap holding them back.
  • Scored on our server, so the room view and the record are the source of truth.
germanysummit.sirahagents.com/reality-check
Module 02 / CV & LinkedIn Lab

What a German recruiter sees in 8 seconds

  • Upload a CV as PDF or Word, paste the German job they want.
  • Returns an ATS match score, a recruiter-style keyword checklist, German-format checks, and the weak bullets, named not rewritten.
  • A second tab does the same for their LinkedIn profile.
  • Re-analyze after fixes and it shows the lift from the last attempt.
germanysummit.sirahagents.com/cv-lab
Module 03 / 90-Day Roadmap

Twelve weeks, front-loaded on the weak spots

  • Builds a week-by-week plan from their score, front-loaded on their two weakest dimensions.
  • Mirrors the physical 90-Day Visual Roadmap, and points to the Roadmap Reveal and the sprint.
  • Downloadable as a PDF. Built once per person, so it stays their plan unless the team re-enables it.
germanysummit.sirahagents.com/roadmap

07

Admin controls

germanysummit.sirahagents.com/room/records, opens with the admin key

Your private control room. Every entry shows name, email, phone, session, score, tier, archetype, the modules they finished, and the time in IST.

Retake control

  • Allow or lock one person. A finished Reality Check locks that person by default, matched on email and on phone.
  • Allow everyone to retake. One global switch. Turn it on for the arrival and end-of-day pulses so the room can score again, then turn it back off.
  • Reset all locks. Clears everything back to a fresh state.

Roadmaps and records

  • Unlock a new roadmap. Plans are one per person. Grant a regenerate when someone genuinely needs a fresh one. It is used up on the next build.
  • Delete a record. Remove a test entry or a duplicate.
  • Treat the admin key like a password. Keep it off the screen and out of public hands.
08

What the team must provide: the AI key

  • The CV diagnosis, the LinkedIn read, and the 90-day roadmap are generated by Anthropic's Claude AI.
  • B2 must provide an Anthropic API key and pay for usage. It is a paid, pay-as-you-go service, billed by Anthropic, not by us.
  • The Reality Check score works without it. The three AI tools will not run without a funded key.

Set it up once:

  • Create an account at console.anthropic.com and add a billing method.
  • Create an API key.
  • Paste it into the app hosting (Vercel) as ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and redeploy.
Rough cost for the day

Each analysis costs a few US cents. For a 300-person event where people run the CV Lab and the roadmap, budget roughly USD 30 to 60 (about 2,500 to 5,000 rupees), with a buffer. Built-in prompt caching keeps this low.

Keep a small top-up on the account so it never runs dry mid-event. Treat the key like a password.
