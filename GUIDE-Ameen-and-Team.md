# Germany Readiness Suite
### Quick guide for Ameen and the B2 team

**What it is**
- A web app for the Germany Career Summit. One link, three tools, one score.
- Live at **germanysummit.sirahagents.com**

**Why it exists (the objective)**
- Turn "Am I ready for Germany?" into a real number, a clear gap, and a plan, in under 10 minutes.
- Moves every attendee from inspiration to action while they are still in the room.
- Hands the coach a warm, qualified conversation: the app exposes the gap, the sprint closes it.

**The three tools**
- **Reality Check:** 10 questions. Gives a 0 to 100 Germany Readiness Score, a 5-point radar, their archetype, and the one gap holding them back.
- **CV and LinkedIn Lab:** they upload a CV (PDF or Word). Gives an ATS score, a recruiter-style keyword check against a real German job, and what is weak. The LinkedIn tab does the same for their profile. It diagnoses, it never rewrites.
- **90-Day Roadmap:** a week-by-week plan built from their score, front-loaded on their two weakest areas. Downloadable as PDF.

**The golden rule**
- The app diagnoses. The coach and the sprint deliver.
- It never writes their CV or hands over the finished answer. It shows the gap, then points to you.

**Running it on event day (three moments)**
- Each moment has a clean link. Share it as a link, or turn it into a QR code, whichever fits the screen or the message.
  - Pre-event (email, funnel): `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/reality-check`
  - On arrival (registration screen, WhatsApp): `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/arrival`
  - End of day (closing slide, WhatsApp): `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/end-of-day`
- The link decides the phase, not the clock. The short links tag it automatically, no extra setup.
- For a printed QR at the desk or a QR on the closing slide, just point the QR at the matching link above.
- To capture a real before/after, turn on "Allow everyone to retake" in admin during the arrival and end-of-day pulses, so people can score again with the same email.

**The big screen (on stage)**
- `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/room` shows the room live: average score, archetypes, the gaps everyone shares.
- Tabs filter by Pre-event, On arrival, End of day.
- Use it as a hook: "this room's average readiness is 54, and the number one gap is Strategy."

**Admin, private**
- `germanysummit.sirahagents.com/room/records` opens with your admin key.
- Browse every entry, allow a retake, unlock a new roadmap, or remove a record.
- Keep the admin key off the screen and out of public hands.

**Do this on the day**
- Hand out the right QR at the right moment.
- Flash the /room screen between sessions to build momentum.
- When someone is rattled by their score, that is the cue: introduce the sprint.
